# 🌑 DuskVeil — A Jack the Ripper Pixel Art Adventure

> *"The world is full of obvious things which nobody by any chance ever observes."*
> — Sherlock Holmes

> *"To discover the truth, one must first accept being lost in it."*

**DuskVeil** is a 2D pixel art adventure game developed by **Groupe 1 — Arcani Vaganti**, inspired by the Jack the Ripper mysteries. Navigate the thin line between reality and illusion in a dark, atmospheric Victorian world.

---

## 🎭 The Story

DuskVeil plunges the player into a world where truth flickers in the uncertain light of dusk.

- **Dusk** — the fragile instant between day and night, where clarity slowly fades into shadow and doubt.
- **Veil** — the barrier between the real and the imagined, between what we think we see and what hides behind.

The goal is not simply to find Jack the Ripper — it is to find yourself in a world where truth never reveals itself without a fight. The game explores the blurry frontiers between reality and illusion, memory, identity, and doubt, targeting young adults and curious minds drawn to deep, symbolic, immersive narratives.

## 🎮 Gameplay Overview

### Game Modes
- **One Player** — Solo investigation mode
- **Multi Player** — Collaborative mystery solving

### Controls
- Mouse & Keyboard
- Joystick supported

### Player Avatars
- Avatar I & Avatar II — two playable characters with full pixel art animations (Walk, Attack, Jump, Idle)

## 🧩 Enigmas

The game features 4 distinct puzzle chapters:

1. **London's Riddle** — Unravel the streets of Victorian London
2. **Orphanage's Riddle** — Face the secrets of a haunted past
3. **Mirror Puzzle** — Confront illusion and reflection
4. **Final Truth** — Uncover what lies behind the veil
